# Aceve Onboard — user guide

*For Aceve customers preparing their onboarding. This guide follows portal
version 0.1.0.*

## What is this?

Aceve Onboard is the portal where you prepare your start-up and your data
migration — at your own pace, even before your system exists. Several people
at your company can work in parallel, everything is saved continuously, and
you and Aceve see the same status at all times.

The flow has two phases and a finish:

1. **Preparation** — company details, your business, superusers, webinars and
   the responsibility split.
2. **Data migration** — your data, category by category (chart of accounts,
   users, price lists …), with a method choice per category.
3. **Submit & book go-live** — unlocks when everything above is done.

## Getting started

1. Open the link you received and pick a language (SV/EN) at the top of the
   sidebar.
2. The **Welcome** step states why you are here — your signed agreement with
   Aceve. Click **Get started** to open the rest of the flow.
3. Work your way down the sidebar. One step at a time is marked active — it is
   always the natural next step. The percentage shows how far you have come.

## The preparation steps

- **Company details** — fields marked *"Prefilled from Salesforce"* are things
  we already know. Review them instead of re-entering. Country and language
  decide which product and which trainings you are guided to.
- **Your business & background** — describe your business. If you come from a
  previous system (e.g. Byggsamordnaren), name an IT contact for the data
  export.
- **Superusers** — add at least two people. The counter shows how many valid
  rows you have (valid = first name, last name and a correct email).
- **Trainings** — one check per person per training. All are required. When a
  booking link exists it is shown next to the training name.
- **Responsibility split** — read the document and confirm the three points.

## The mapping

After the preparation comes a mapping step (e.g. which finance system you use
and how the chart of accounts and customer register should come in). Two
things are worth knowing:

- **The mapping does not block the data steps** — you can work on both in
  parallel. It does need to be completed before you submit.
- **Your answers steer the data steps**: if you choose that a category is
  fetched via integration, it leaves your to-do list and is marked "Fetched
  via integration". If you change your mind, the category comes back.

## The data migration

- **Migration plan** — choose a method per data category: GI template, Excel,
  integration, or *Skip* (then done manually after go-live — a perfectly fine
  choice). You can also delegate: pick a responsible person per category.
- **Category steps** — each category shows which fields are expected
  (highlighted fields are mandatory) before you start, so you can see what
  "done" means. The import function with automatic column matching arrives in
  an upcoming version — your method choices and planning are saved already.

## Submitting

**Submit & book go-live** stays locked until every step is done — and lists
exactly which steps remain. The same page shows a **summary of everything you
entered**, section by section, with a *Change* link back to each step. When
everything is green, submit, and we will come back with a go-live time.

## FAQ

**Do we have to do everything at once?** No. Everything is saved when you
press Save, and you can return at any time.

**Can several people work at the same time?** Yes — that is the idea.
Delegate data categories to different people in the Migration plan.

**What's new in the portal?** Click *What's new* at the bottom of the sidebar.
